First time here?

  1. Head for Al Forsan

    Al Forsan International Sports Resort is in Khalifa City, near Abu Dhabi Golf Club: about 10 minutes from the airport and 15 from Yas Island. Search "Adam's Beer Kitchen at Al Forsan" in Google Maps.

  2. Park at the resort

    Use the car parks just east of the clubhouse.

  3. Find the main clubhouse

    It's the big building next to the karting track, a short walk from the wakeboarding lakes.

  4. Grab a table

    Inside the hall for the screens, or outside by the play area.
