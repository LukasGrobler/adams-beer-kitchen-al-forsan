Our story

Adam's, off the island.

Adam's Beer Kitchen started on Al Reem Island. Al Forsan is the second Abu Dhabi branch: the same casual hangout, cold beers and comfort food, off the island and inside one of the city's biggest sports resorts.

We're in the main clubhouse at Al Forsan International Sports Resort, next to the karting track and a short walk from the wakeboarding lakes. That makes us the place people go after the activity: teams coming off the paintball field, families after a morning at the lake, and Khalifa City neighbours who just want a good burger and the match.

Our line on Google says it in six words: good food, great drinks, better vibes.
