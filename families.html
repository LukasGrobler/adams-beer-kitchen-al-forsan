Families welcome

Daytime at the clubhouse is family time: a kids' menu, a small outdoor play area and a team that makes birthdays feel special. Families are welcome for food during the day.

Open todayHappy hour every day

What families get

  • A kids' menu

    Nuggets and chips are the favourite, and the grown-ups get the full menu.

  • A small outdoor play area

    So the adults can sit down while the kids run around.

  • Family Fridays

    Kids eat free with every adult main till 6PM, and a family film on the big screen at 4PM.

  • Best times to come

    Lunch and early evening. For late event nights, check with the team first.

Plan a kids' party

Tell us the date and the numbers. The team will sort the food, a table near the play area, and the film for the big screen if you'd like one.
